▸ How It Works

Your BTC follows the power law price model — the most-likely long-run projection. Setting Today's BTC Price anchors the curve so it passes through that point right now; the future path keeps its power-law shape from there.

Each retirement year opens a new Ledn loan at 50% LTV. The new draw funds your annual budget plus the interest accrued on all prior loans — so old tranches stay flat at their original principal and never compound. Only the newest year's loan grows.

Optional: set Buy More BTC % to spend a slice of each year's borrow on more BTC instead of expenses. Loan size stays the same — you just live a bit leaner in exchange for compounding stack growth.

APR decays over time from your Start rate toward your Floor rate as the BTC-backed lending market matures (S&P-rated platforms, institutional capital, more competition). This compresses interest service costs over the decades.

As price rises, each tranche's LTV decays. When it falls under 30%, surplus collateral is reclaimed back to your unpledged stack — sats stay yours, loans stay open.

The Borrow column shows that year's new draw (budget + interest serviced). Debt shows total outstanding across all tranches. Agg LTV is total debt ÷ total stack value — not per-tranche. Tap a year to pause that year's borrow. Tap Debt or Unpledged ₿ to override.
