▸ Where To Point Your Skepticism

You can't predict the future, but you can know which of your guesses matters most. This holds your plan at its baseline, then changes each input ±25% one at a time and re-runs the volatility simulation (pause defense on).

The result is a plain-English ranked list: which assumptions actually decide whether the plan works (tagged CRITICAL / IMPORTANT), and which barely move the outcome and you can stop worrying about (MINOR / IGNORE).

Typically stack size and budget dominate, while the loan rate barely matters — the opposite of where most people focus their attention. The classic tornado chart is still there, tucked below the list for anyone who wants it.
