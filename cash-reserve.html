▸ Why This Exists

The whole suite's headline finding is that the borrow strategy only survives volatility if you skip borrowing in down years and cover those years from outside cash. But nothing told you how much cash that reserve needs to be. This does.

It runs 1,000 volatile futures with the pause defense active. On each path it tracks the total real dollars actually pulled from the reserve to cover paused (down) years — including back-to-back crash streaks. Then it reports the reserve size that would have covered 50% / 75% / 90% / 99% of those futures.

Size to the 90% tier at minimum. The 99% tier protects against brutal multi-year crash streaks early in retirement — the exact scenario that destroys the unprotected plan.
