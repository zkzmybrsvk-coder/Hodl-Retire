▸ The Synthesis

This is the whole suite in one tool. It runs the borrow-early, sell-late hybrid across hundreds of volatile price paths (power law = mean, real volatility layered on), with the down-year pause defense active in the borrow phase.

It scans every possible switch year and reports the survival probability of each — then picks the one most robust to a random future, not the one that only looks best if the power law is exact.

The switch is a real liquidation event: at that year you repay all loans by selling BTC at whatever the path price is (incl. tax). A switch landing in a crash is costly — which is exactly why testing it under volatility, not a smooth curve, matters.
