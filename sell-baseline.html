▸ The Honest Question

The borrow strategy sounds clever, but is it actually better than just selling a little BTC each year? Selling has no interest, no liquidation risk, no counterparty risk — you just lose sats permanently.

This runs both on the same power-law price path. Just Sell sells exactly enough BTC each year to cover your budget. Borrow runs the full HODL&Retire mechanic. Same prices, same budget — which leaves you more sats?

Capital-gains tax is modeled on the sell side (it makes selling worse). Borrowing is genuinely tax-free, which is a large part of its real-world appeal.
